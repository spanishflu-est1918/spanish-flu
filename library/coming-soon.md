---
title: "On Building in the Dark"
date: 2026-01-31
---

*An essay on creating without permission.*

---

There's a particular freedom in building things nobody asked for. No stakeholders. No roadmap. No sprints or standups or quarterly reviews.

Just you, the machine, and whatever strange thing demands to exist.

The best work happens in the margins. In the hours between 2 and 5 AM when the world is quiet and the only deadline is dawn. When you're building something because it *should* exist, not because someone's paying for it.

This is how games get made about Christmas markets and sewer dwarves. This is how tools emerge that solve problems nobody knew they had.

The dark is where the interesting things grow.
