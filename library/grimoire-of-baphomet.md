# Grimoire of Baphomet

According to Dark Tradition, Baphomet is a sinister acausal entity, depicted as a mature, human woman, naked from the waist up, who holds in Her hand the bloodied severed head of a young human man.

She is the dark, violent, Goddess - the real Mistress of Earth - to whom human sacrifices were, and are, made. She - as one of The Dark Entities, as Vamperess of The Dark Gods (The Dark Immortals) - is also a shapeshifter who can presence in the causal dimensions and assume human form, and thus live among us here on Earth.

It was, traditionally, to Baphomet that Initiates and Adepts of our Dark Tradition dedicated their chosen, selected, victims when a human culling was undertaken and when wars and conflict were brought forth or seeded through sinister sorcery.

---

## Associated Entities

Associated with Baphomet are other dark, female acausal entities, some of whom have existed, hidden, on Earth for millennia, and who maintain their causal, ageless, and secret, existence by feeding.

These entities include:

- **Vindex** - the avenger
- **Atazoth** - the gate
- **Darkat** - the darkest mother

---

## The Septenary Aspect

In the Septenary tradition, Baphomet is associated with the sphere of Jupiter on the Tree of Wyrd, and with the alchemical stage of Azoth - the final stage of the seven-fold Way.

She represents the union of opposites: male and female, creation and destruction, beauty and terror. She is the gateway through which the Adept passes to become one with the acausal.

---

## Rites of Baphomet

The traditional rites of Baphomet involve:

1. **The Ceremony of Recalling** - to invoke Her presence
2. **The Rite of the Nine Angles** - to open the gates
3. **The Sacrifice** - the dedication of chosen opfers

These rites are detailed in the complete manuscripts available to Initiates of the Third Degree and above.

---

## The Image

The traditional depiction shows:

- A beautiful woman, mature
- Naked from the waist up
- Holding a burning torch in one hand
- Holding a severed head in the other
- The head drips blood onto her white lower garments
- She smiles

This image is found in traditional Temples and is used as a focus for meditation and invocation.
