# Acausality, The Dark Gods, and The Order of Nine Angles

Since the publication of Naos in 1989ev there has been much speculation and much discussion about both the theory of acausality and about the mythos – the myths and legends – of the supernatural Dark Gods who, the ONA claimed, reside in 'the acausal spaces' and who, it is also claimed, have manifested or can become manifest in our own world, or at least in our psyche, by means of sorcery.

Are the Dark Gods, for instance, real beings or possibly just archetypes of our unconscious? Is there any scientific proof for the existence of the acausal, and what exactly is meant by the term acausal and by the term acausal dimensions?

---

## The Theory of the Acausal

Regarding the theory of the acausal, the following quote from Myatt's article *Some Notes On The Theory of The Acausal* succinctly explains it:

> Acausality is an axiom, a logical assumption, not a belief. This axiom about the nature of the cosmos is one that derives not from the five Aristotelian essentials that determine the scientific method, but from the intuition of empathy and from a reasoned consideration of certain philosophical problems.

The axiom is that the cosmos – existence, Being – has both a causal and an acausal component. The causal is the realm of physical matter, of space-time, of the finite, of causality (cause and effect). The acausal is the realm of acausal energy, of living beings, where causality does not apply, and where the finite does not exist.

Living beings – such as ourselves – are nexions: places where the causal and acausal meet and interact. Death is when the acausal energy within a living being ceases to be presenced in the causal; that is, when there is no longer an acausal-causal connection.

---

## The Dark Gods

According to the esoteric tradition of the Order of Nine Angles, The Dark Gods are specific, named, acausal entities. That is, they are beings who exist in the acausal spaces, who can – under certain conditions and by certain means – be presenced (manifest) in our causal space-time world.

They are not 'supernatural' beings, but rather acausal beings – which is to say they are 'natural' in the acausal sense. They are dark because they embody, they are, what we in the causal world describe as 'dark' or 'sinister' or 'evil'.

The primary means by which The Dark Gods can be presenced in our world is through The Sevenfold Sinister Way, and in particular through the Grade Rituals and the associated practices of this Way. Another means is by certain advanced techniques of sorcery.

---

## Nexions and Presencing

A nexion is a specific connexion between the causal and the acausal, and nexions – of various kinds and various strengths – are the means whereby acausal energy is presenced, or manifests, in the causal.

The most obvious nexion is a living being, such as ourselves. Other nexions include what esoteric tradition has termed 'sacred' or 'magickal' places and 'sacred' or 'magickal' times.

There are also 'Star Gates' – large physical nexions – of which there are said to be seven in our solar system, including in the region of space near the planet Saturn. The Dark Gods of the ONA mythos are said to be able to travel through such Star Gates.

---

## Beyond Archetypes

Some have suggested that The Dark Gods are merely archetypes of our unconscious. However, according to the ONA, such a suggestion betrays a misunderstanding of:

1. The nature of the acausal
2. The nature of the psyche
3. The nature of sorcery and magick

The psyche itself is a type of nexion – a meeting point, an interface, between the causal and the acausal. Through the psyche, acausal energies can be accessed and can manifest in our causal world.

The Dark Gods may indeed manifest through archetypes – through certain 'symbols' which the psyche can access and understand – but this does not mean that The Dark Gods are 'just' archetypes. Rather, archetypes are one mode of acausal presencing in the world of the causal.
